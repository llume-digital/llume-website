What we do

Useful technology for
real problems.

llume is a technology consultancy based in Oviedo, Spain. We help companies simplify processes and solve specific challenges with automation, tool integration, custom applications, cloud solutions and computer vision.

How we can help

We start with how you work and the problem you want to solve. Then we assess the options, explain their trade-offs and propose a scope that makes sense for your company.

Automation

We reduce manual steps and connect tasks so work can move forward with less friction.

Integration and cloud

We connect tools and data, or plan a move to the cloud around your needs.

Custom applications

We design platforms and tools around your processes and users.

Computer vision

We assess whether cameras and vision models can help with a specific task.

A clear way to work

Before starting, we agree on goals, scope, deliverables, timeline and price. During the project, we share progress and decisions. At handover, we document what was delivered and clarify next steps.

Technology should fit the way people work and provide an improvement you can explain.
