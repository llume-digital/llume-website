Training

Help your team get the most out of AI.

Hands-on training in three sessions, designed around your company. First we understand how you work, then we run two workshops with exercises on your real tasks. It works with any AI tool.

  • In person, at your company
  • Online, live
  • For the whole team or by area

How the training works

Session 1Before the training

We get to know how you work

We talk to your team to understand what you do, which tools you use and which tasks take up most of your time. We use that to prepare the workshop exercises.

  • Day-to-day tasks and processes
  • Tools you already use
  • Your team's starting level
  • Real cases to practise with
Session 2Hands-on workshop · 3 h

Working with AI every day

The fundamentals to use AI wisely and get value from day one.

  • How models work and what they can (and can't) do
  • Choosing the right model and effort level for each task
  • Asking well: instructions, context and examples
  • Managing conversations: branching, rewinding and resuming
  • Checking results and avoiding errors
  • Exercises with your own emails, documents and reports
Session 3Hands-on workshop · 3 h

From assistant to agent

Taking AI beyond chat: so it knows your company, works with your tools and does tasks for you.

  • Instruction files for agents (AGENTS.md and similar): your company explained to the AI
  • Reusable skills for each role
  • Connecting AI to email, calendar, documents and the CRM
  • Automating repetitive tasks and emails
  • Creating documents and small tools without coding
  • Security and data protection

And at the end, a guide to keep going on your own

A document adapted to your company with everything covered in the workshops:

  • The instructions and skills created for your roles
  • Prompt templates for your most common tasks
  • Which model and effort level to use for each kind of task
  • Good practice and usage rules for the team

It is tool-independent: we work with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Microsoft Copilot or whichever you use.

What is more, the EU AI Act requires companies that use AI to take measures to ensure a sufficient level of AI literacy among their staff (Article 4).
