Trip management platform for Proactive Future

proactivefuture.eu

The problem

An association that runs trips managed registrations, payments and participants across spreadsheets, forms and several disconnected platforms.

The solution

Its own web platform, with frontend and backend, that centralises the data, takes payments online and organises participants and coordinators in one place.

Before

Registrations, payments and participant lists cross-checked by hand across several tools.

After

Participants sign up and pay on their own, and the team sees every trip in real time.

Context

Proactive Future is an association that organises trips, exchanges and European projects for young people. Every trip involves registrations, limited places, staged payments and a lot of communication with participants and coordinators.

The challenge

Information was spread across spreadsheets, forms, emails and different payment gateways. Knowing who had paid, how many places were left or what was still pending meant cross-checking data by hand, with the risk of errors and duplicates.

What we did

  • A public website in Spanish and English with the catalogue of trips and open opportunities.
  • Online registration with user accounts and card payments in stages: deposit and balance.
  • A management dashboard for the team and coordinators, with participants, places, payments and documents for each trip.
  • Automatic notifications: payment reminders, departure countdowns and availability alerts.
  • Participant reviews after each trip, to measure and improve every edition.

Technology

  • React
  • TypeScript
  • Node.js
  • PostgreSQL
  • Stripe
  • Cloudflare

The result

The association's entire operation lives in a single system. Participants sign up and pay on their own, and the team sees the status of every trip in real time without cross-checking spreadsheets.
