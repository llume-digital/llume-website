Hard-hat and safety vest detection on site

The problem

Checking around the clock that health and safety measures are followed is impossible for one person.

The solution

A vision system identifies hard hats and safety vests visible in the scene.

This video shows a demonstration of hard-hat and vest detection on a construction site. The system highlights people and the protective equipment it recognises in each frame.

Application

This detection can support site supervision. Alerts, incident logs and image handling depend on the installation and its privacy requirements.
