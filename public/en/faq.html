How can llume help my company?

We look at tasks and processes and may suggest automation, tool integration, cloud migration, custom applications or computer vision. The right approach depends on the problem and the tools you already use.

How do we get started?

You can request a free, no-obligation 30-minute introductory call. Tell us about the challenge and we’ll assess whether we can help. If there is a fit, we’ll prepare a proposal with scope, deliverables, timeline and price before work begins.

How much does a project cost and how long does it take?

It depends on scope, complexity and integrations. The proposal sets out the price and timeline; we do not promise a standard schedule or saving for every project.

Do I need technical skills?

No. We start with how your team works and explain the options in plain language. If a solution requires changes to your systems, we agree in advance what access and decisions are needed.

What do we receive at the end?

Deliverables, documentation, training and support are described in the proposal. We also clarify maintenance and any recurring costs, such as third-party licences or hosting.

Who owns the code, and what about licences?

The proposal and contract should set out ownership and handover of the work, along with licences for third-party components or services. We clarify this before starting.

How do you handle our data?

Before accessing project data, we agree what information is needed, who can access it and which external services are involved. Applicable terms and ways to exercise your rights are described in the Privacy Policy.

Do you offer team training?

Yes. We can prepare practical training on digital tools and AI, tailored to your team’s tasks and experience. Format, content and duration are agreed in the proposal.
