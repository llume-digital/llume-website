02

Custom computer vision

If it can be seen, it can usually be detected, counted or classified. We build computer vision systems tailored to your case, often on the cameras you already have.

Examples

Industry and logistics

  • Defects in boxes, packaging or parts on the line
  • Sorting products by type or format
  • Counting and traceability with no manual work

Safety and prevention

  • Use of helmets, vests and other protective equipment
  • Access to restricted or dangerous areas
  • Risky behaviour or violent situations

Mobility and transport

  • Prohibited items on board, such as e-scooters
  • Harsh braking and risky driving
  • Abandoned objects in stations or vehicles

Infrastructure

  • Cracks and potholes in the road from vehicles on the move
  • Condition of signage and street furniture
  • Regular inspections with a photographic record

How we do it

  1. 01

    We study your case

    What needs detecting, where and with which cameras.

  2. 02

    We train the model

    With real images of your process.

  3. 03

    We put it to work

    On your premises or in the cloud, with instant alerts.

  4. 04

    We measure and improve

    A dashboard of detections and tuning to cut false alarms.

Every system is designed with privacy in mind: only what is needed is processed and, where possible, images never leave your premises.
